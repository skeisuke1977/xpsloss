This is a compilation of energy loss functions (ELF) for selected materials.
The ELF data describes the dielectric properties of a material.
It may be applied to calculate energy loss processes of fast electrons moving in the material as well as in geometries met in XPS and AES (as e.g. with the QUEELS-software that can be downloaded at: doi: 10.5281/zenodo.6022426)

The ELF is expanded in a sum of oscillators.

Data structure:
For each material, a reference to the original data is given.
After a line with @ the ELF data follows in lines with the following meaning

1. Excitation parameters ac, bc to model core excitations (see Penn, J. Elec. Spectr., 9(1976)29).
When ac = 0.001 and bc = -2: no data was available. 

2. Energy gap (eV)

3. Refractive Index

in the next lines, are the parameters that describe the oscillators: one line for each oscillator

4. energy position (eV), intensity (eV^2), width (eV), alpha
5.
6.
.
.
Where alpha is the momentum dispersion (=1 for a free electron metal, and = 0-0.1 for the valence electrons in large band gap materials and for core levels)
